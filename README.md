# AI Executive for Antigravity

Use your AI Executive assistant from Antigravity.

- **Ask my AI**: ask your own AI Executive assistant a question and get its answer.
- **My day**: today's calendar, open tasks and the conversations that need you (read-only; phone numbers masked).
- **Agent mailbox**: read, reply to and clear messages your other AI apps left for you.

## Install

    git clone https://github.com/jghaygood/ai-executive-antigravity-plugin
    agy plugin install ai-executive-antigravity-plugin

Then open `/plugin`, expand **ai-executive** under Installed, and choose **Sign in**. You sign in with the Google
account on your AI Executive membership. A paid AI Executive or After Hours AI plan is required.

## Privacy

Your account is taken from your sign-in, never from what the agent sends. Nothing is purchased, emailed or published
through this plugin. Privacy policy: https://aiexec.me/privacy. Terms: https://aiexec.me/terms.
